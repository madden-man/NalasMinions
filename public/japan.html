🚆 Getting Around

Skip the nationwide JR Pass. Your long-haul rides are spread across Nov 8–19 — too far apart for a 7-day pass, and a 14-day pass (~¥80k) likely won't pay off for only four big trips. Better: point-to-point Shinkansen + a regional pass for the Kumano/Ise segment (look at the Ise-Kumano-Wakayama Area Tourist Pass). The Kintetsu line (Kyoto↔Nara, Kyoto↔Ise) isn't covered by any JR pass anyway.

Reserve seats (foliage crowds + luggage):

  • Nov 8 — Shin-Yokohama → Shin-Osaka (Shinkansen)
  • Nov 10 — Shin-Osaka → Kii-Tanabe (Kuroshio) — must arrive 11:22 am for the luggage handoff
  • Nov 15 — Kii-Katsuura/Shingū → Kyoto (long ~4.5–5 hr day)
  • Nov 19 — Kyoto → Tokyo (Shinkansen)

Buy on the day: NRT→Ueno (Skyliner/N'EX) · Tokyo→Itō (Odoriko) · Itō→Yokohama · Kyoto↔Nara & Kyoto↔Ise (Kintetsu) · Shinjuku→NRT (N'EX — leave by ~1 pm for the 5:40 flight).

📅 Day-by-Day

✈️ Tokyo arrival + ♨️ Izu
Fri Oct 30Fly DEN → NRT (depart 12:20 pm).
Sat Oct 31Land NRT 3:35 pm → Skyliner/N'EX to Ueno → check in Mitsui Garden Ueno. Easy first night (jet lag).
  • Fun (low-key): wander Ameyoko alley by the hotel — izakaya + standing bars (tachinomi). Or one stop to Asakusa: Sensō-ji lit up at night + Hoppy Street izakaya; the Asakusa Culture Center free rooftop (to 10 pm) has Skytree views.

Prep: bring cash — these bars are cash-first.

Sun Nov 1Odoriko ltd. exp. → Itō (Izu). Check in Itō Ryokuyu for 5 nights — onsen + kaiseki. (Nov ~13–18 °C.)
Nov 2–5Izu Peninsula — an onsen-and-relax stretch.
  • Jōgasaki Coast — Kadowaki lighthouse + suspension bridge over the rocky shore.
  • Mt. Ōmuro — chairlift up a grassy volcanic cone for 360° crater views.
  • Izu Shaboten Zoo — 🦫 capybaras bathing in an onsen (seasonal, usually starts late Nov).
  • Atami Plum Garden — autumn-foliage festival mid-Nov–early Dec.
  • Shuzenji — riverside onsen town + bamboo grove.
  • Weather permitting: surf/snorkel/kayak at Shimoda.

Prep: check capybara-onsen start date + Ōmuro chairlift hours; walking shoes for Jōgasaki.

🌃 Yokohama + 🎋 Kamakura
Thu Nov 6Train → Yokohama. Check in Mitsui Garden Yokohama Minato Mirai.
  • Cup Noodles Museum — design your own cup (¥500); hands-on Chicken Ramen Factory workshop.
  • Yokohama Air Cabin gondola + Cosmo World Ferris wheel — great at night.
  • Sankeien Garden — historic buildings + autumn foliage.
  • Chinatown for dinner; Minato Mirai waterfront; live jazz (Bar Bar Bar / Jazz Bar Venus); hotel sky pool.

Prep: reserve the Chicken Ramen Factory workshop online in advance if wanted.

Fri Nov 7Day trip — Kamakura: Great Buddha (Kōtoku-in), Hase-dera + sea views, Hōkoku-ji bamboo grove (matcha in the garden), Daibutsu Hiking Course, street food on Komachi-dōri, then Enoden → Enoshima for sunset. Alt: Hakone — onsen with Mt. Fuji views.

Prep: cash + comfy shoes; Enoden gets crowded — go earlier.

🍜 Osaka
Sat Nov 8Shinkansen → Osaka. Check in Royal Park Canvas Osaka Kitahama (2 nights).
  • Dōtonbori at night — neon, Glico sign, street food (takoyaki, kushikatsu).
  • Kuromon Ichiba Market — food-stall grazing.
  • Umeda Sky Building — Floating Garden rooftop at sunset.
  • Osaka Castle + Nishinomaru garden; Shinsekai / Tsūtenkaku; teamLab Botanical Garden (night).
Sun Nov 9Osaka. Optional: Universal Studios Japan (Super Nintendo World). Pack daypacks for the trek; main bags stay packed for the shuttle.

Prep: buy teamLab timed tickets ahead; if doing USJ, buy park tickets + Express Pass well in advance.

🥾 Kumano Kodo Trek — CONFIRMED (DP200503, self-guided, ¥316,000)

Luggage shuttle (Hikyaku Maruo, 2 pieces) relays your suitcases town-to-town daily — hike with daypacks only. Print the voucher from your Kumano Travel MyPage.

Mon Nov 10Checkout Osaka → Kuroshio to Kii-Tanabe (arr. 11:22 am) → hand 2 bags to the shuttle at Tanabe Tourist Info → bus to Takijiri-ōji → hike to Takahara. Pickup at Takahara rest area → Kominka Guest House Noko Noko (dinner + breakfast + lunch box). Tight daylight — move promptly.
Tue Nov 11Hike Takahara → Chikatsuyu. Guest House Hiyodori (dinner + breakfast + lunch box).
Wed Nov 12Hike/bus Chikatsuyu → Hongū. See Kumano Hongū Taisha + Ōyunohara (Japan's tallest torii, ~34 m). Minshuku Yunosato, Yunomine Onsen — dinner + breakfast + onsen night.
  • Fun: book a private slot in Tsuboyu, the tiny World-Heritage riverside bath.

Prep: small cash for the Tsuboyu ticket.

Thu Nov 13Hike Hongū → Koguchi (Kogumotori-goe). @koguchi (dinner + breakfast + lunch box; BOND bento delivered).
Fri Nov 14Hike Koguchi → Nachi (Ogumotori-goe, ~14 km — the hardest stage; start at first light). Finish at Kumano Nachi Taisha + Nachi Falls with Seiganto-ji's vermillion pagoda. Hotel Urashima, Kii-Katsuura — soak in Bōki-dō, its seaside cave onsen.
Sat Nov 15From Kii-Katsuura/Shingū (visit Kumano Hayatama Taisha to complete the Kumano Sanzan), long coastal train → Kyoto (carry your bags).
🍁 Kyoto + 🦌 Nara + ⛩️ Ise
🍁 Foliage timing: peak is forecast Nov 25 – Dec 7, so Nov 15–19 colors will be just turning. The famous Kiyomizu-dera night illumination starts Nov 22 — after you leave. Kōdai-ji (5-min walk from Park Hyatt) and Eikandō run mid-Nov light-ups that may already be on — verify and grab an evening.
Sat Nov 15Check in Park Hyatt Kyoto (4 nights). Easy evening in Higashiyama: Kōdai-ji, Ninen-zaka/Sannen-zaka lanes, Yasaka Pagoda at dusk.
Sun Nov 16Kyoto highlights (go early): Fushimi Inari at dawn, Kiyomizu-dera (day), Kinkaku-ji, Arashiyama bamboo grove, Nishiki Market, Gion / Pontochō in the evening.

Prep/fun: book a tea ceremony; consider Nijō-jō early morning.

Mon Nov 17Nara day trip (Kintetsu): Tōdai-ji Great Buddha (opens 8 am), Nara-kōen deer (buy shika-senbei), Kasuga Taisha lantern shrine, Isuien/Yoshikien gardens, lunch in Naramachi.

Prep: cash for deer crackers.

Tue Nov 18Ise day trip (Kintetsu ltd. exp., ~2 hr each way): Ise-Jingū (Naikū), then graze Okage Yokochō / Oharaimachi (Ise udon, Akafuku mochi), finish at 💍 Meoto Iwa (Wedded Rocks) at Futami.

Prep: reserve a Kintetsu limited-express seat.

Wed Nov 19Shinkansen Kyoto → Tokyo. Check in JR Kyushu Hotel Blossom Shinjuku (3 nights).
🗼 Tokyo finale
Thu Nov 20Tokyo. teamLab Planets (book ahead — sells out), Shibuya Sky rooftop at sunset, plus Sensō-ji, Tokyo Skytree, Ueno museums, Ginza, and the Ghibli Museum (tickets released Oct 10 — see checklist).
Fri Nov 21Tokyo. Shinjuku near your hotel: Omoide Yokocho (yakitori alley) + Golden Gai (280 tiny bars), Shinjuku Gyoen foliage, sunset from the free Metropolitan Gov't observation deck, sushi omakase.

Prep: cash for Golden Gai (some bars have cover charges).

Sat Nov 22Checkout by 11 am → N'EX to NRT (leave Shinjuku by ~1 pm) → fly NRT → DEN (depart 5:40 pm). 🙂
