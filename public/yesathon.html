Recipes

Seven meals, about two hours of cooking across the whole weekend.

Fri 6:30p

Pepperoni hot-honey pizza

20 minOven
  1. Heat the oven to 425°F, or whatever the box says.
  2. Cover the frozen pizza with pepperoni, right to the edges.
  3. Bake it straight on the rack for 13–16 minutes, until the cheese bubbles and the pepperoni curls into little cups.
  4. Drizzle hot honey over the top, wait 2 minutes, and slice.
Sat 9:00a

Blueberry pancakes and bacon

25 minSheet pan, skillet
  1. Heat the oven to 400°F. Line a sheet pan with foil, lay out the whole pack of bacon, and bake 18–22 minutes until crisp.
  2. Stir the pancake mix with water per the box and fold in half the blueberries. Lumps are fine.
  3. Melt a little butter in a skillet over medium. Pour quarter-cup scoops; flip when bubbles pop on top, about 2 minutes a side.
  4. Butter, syrup, the rest of the blueberries. Put half the bacon in the fridge for Sunday.
Sat 12:33p

Grilled cheese and tomato soup

12 minSkillet, microwave
  1. Butter the outside of two slices of sourdough and put two slices of cheddar between them.
  2. Cook in a skillet over medium-low with a lid on, 3–4 minutes, until golden. Flip and do the other side without the lid.
  3. Microwave half the carton of soup in a big mug for 2–3 minutes, stirring once.
  4. Cut the sandwich corner to corner and dunk.
Sat 6:48p

Taco night

20 minSkillet
  1. Brown the whole pound of beef in a skillet over medium-high for 6–8 minutes, breaking it up. Pour off extra fat.
  2. Stir in the taco seasoning and 2/3 cup water. Simmer 5 minutes until thick.
  3. Warm tortillas for 20 seconds in the microwave under a damp paper towel.
  4. Beef, cheese, pico, sour cream, avocado, a squeeze of lime. Put half the beef in a container for Sunday.
Sun 8:40a

Breakfast burritos

12 minSkillet
  1. Whisk 3 eggs with a pinch of salt. Melt butter in the skillet over medium-low and stir slowly until just set.
  2. Microwave Saturday’s bacon for 30 seconds.
  3. Fill a tortilla with eggs, bacon, a handful of cheese, and salsa. Roll it up.
  4. Toast it seam-side down in the skillet for a minute so it holds together. Make a second one for Pluto.
Sun 12:28p

Leftover-taco nachos

12 minSheet pan
  1. Heat the oven to 400°F and line a sheet pan with foil.
  2. Spread chips in one layer. Top with the leftover taco beef and a lot of cheese.
  3. Bake 6–8 minutes until the cheese melts.
  4. Finish with pico, sour cream, and the second avocado.
Sun 6:25p

Steak night

30 minSkillet, microwave
  1. Take the steak out 30–45 minutes early. Pat it dry and salt both sides well.
  2. Poke the potato with a fork. Microwave 5 minutes, flip, then 3–5 more until soft.
  3. Get the skillet very hot with a little oil. Steak 4 minutes, flip, 3–4 more for medium-rare on a 1-inch steak (130°F if you have a thermometer). Add a tablespoon of butter for the last minute and spoon it over.
  4. Rest the steak 5 minutes while you toss the Caesar kit. Butter, salt, and sour cream on the potato.
